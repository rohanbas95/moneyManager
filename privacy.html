💰 Money Manager: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

‹ Back to the app

In short

Money Manager is a personal expense tracker that runs entirely in your browser. There is no Money Manager server. Your income, expenses and categories are never sent to the developer, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers.

What is stored and where

  • On your device. Everything you enter (amounts, dates, notes, categories) and your settings are saved in your browser's local storage on that device.
  • In your own Google Drive (optional). If you turn on Google Drive sync, a copy of your data is saved as one file in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, so your devices can stay in step. Only this app can see that folder; it doesn't show in your Drive files, and the developer has no access to it.
  • In your own GitHub repository (optional). If you turn on GitHub sync instead, a copy is saved as a file in the GitHub repository you choose, using an access token you create. The token is saved only in your browser.

Google account data

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for two permissions:

  • Its own app data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata), to read and write the one file that holds your Money Manager data. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Your email address, only to show which account you're signed in with on the Sync screen.

The sign-in token Google gives the app is kept in your browser and expires after about an hour. Your Google data is used only to provide sync for you. It is not sold, shared, used for ads or used to train AI models, and no person reads it.

Money Manager's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. When sync is on, your browser talks directly to Google (sign-in and Drive) or GitHub; their own privacy policies apply to those services. The app doesn't use cookies of its own.

Deleting your data

  • From a device: clear this site's data in your browser settings. Export a backup first from the app's menu if you want to keep it.
  • From Google Drive: open Google Drive on a computer → Settings ⚙️ → Manage apps → Money Manager → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account: go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Money Manager.
  • From GitHub: delete the file or repository you chose, and delete the access token.

Children

Money Manager is not directed at children under 13.
