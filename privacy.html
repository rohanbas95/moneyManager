💰 Money Manager: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

‹ Back to the app

In short

Money Manager is a personal expense tracker that runs in your browser. Your income, expenses, categories, bill photos and Wealth details (accounts, investments, assets, loans) are never sent to the developer, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers. The only exception is the optional shared groups feature: bills you add to a shared group are stored online (in Google Firebase) so the people in that group can see them.

What is stored and where

  • On your device. Everything you enter (amounts, dates, notes, categories, and the bank accounts, investments, assets, loans, insurance and goals in the Wealth tab) and your settings are saved in your browser's local storage on that device.
  • In your own Google Drive (optional). If you turn on Google Drive sync, a copy of your data is saved in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, so your devices can stay in step: one file with your data, plus one file per bill photo you attach to an expense. Only this app can see that folder; it doesn't show in your Drive files, and the developer has no access to it.
  • In your own GitHub repository (optional). If you turn on GitHub sync instead, a copy is saved as a file in the GitHub repository you choose (with bill photos as separate files next to it), using an access token you create. The token is saved only in your browser.
  • Bill photos. A photo you attach to an expense is shrunk and kept on your device, and copied to your Google Drive or GitHub if sync is on. Removing the photo or deleting the expense deletes it there too.
  • Scanning a bill (optional). When you tap "Scan bill", that photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI (through Google Firebase) to read the items and prices, and the result comes back to the app. The photo isn't stored by the app anywhere else. This uses Gemini's free tier, under whose terms Google may use what's sent to improve its services, so don't scan anything you'd consider sensitive.
  • Shared groups (optional). If you sign in with Google to use shared groups, each group's name, its members' names, email addresses and UPI IDs (if they add one), and the bills and payments added to it are stored in a Google Firebase database run for this app, so everyone in the group sees the same thing. Only signed-in members of a group can read or change it. Your own share of a group bill is also added to your personal expenses on your device. Your personal expenses, income and categories are never stored there.

Google account data

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for two permissions:

  • Its own app data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata), to read and write the files that hold your Money Manager data and bill photos. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Your email address, only to show which account you're signed in with on the Sync screen.

The sign-in token Google gives the app is kept in your browser and expires after about an hour. Your Google data is used only to provide sync for you. It is not sold, shared, used for ads or used to train AI models, and no person reads it.

Money Manager's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. When sync is on, your browser talks directly to Google (sign-in and Drive) or GitHub; with shared groups, it also talks to Google Firebase (sign-in and the shared-group database). If you turn on automatic NAV for an investment, the app fetches fund prices from the free public service mfapi.in (AMFI data): only the fund’s scheme code is sent, never your units, amounts or anything about you. If you value gold from today’s rate, the app fetches the gold price from gold-api.com; nothing about you or your gold is sent. Their own privacy policies apply to those services. The app doesn't use cookies of its own.

Deleting your data

  • From a device: clear this site's data in your browser settings. Export a backup first from the app's menu if you want to keep it.
  • From Google Drive: open Google Drive on a computer → Settings ⚙️ → Manage apps → Money Manager → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account: go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Money Manager.
  • From GitHub: delete the file or repository you chose, and delete the access token.
  • From shared groups: leave the group in the app (or, if you created it, delete it). Your name and email, and the bills you were part of, stay visible to the other members, as they are part of their records too.

Children

Money Manager is not directed at children under 13.
